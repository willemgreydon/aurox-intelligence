# ETF Mechanics

## Core Concept

ETF = Exchange-Traded Fund tracking an index or basket of assets.

## Creation / Redemption

- Authorized Participants (APs)
- Create ETF shares via underlying basket
- Arbitrage price deviations

## Types

- Passive ETFs
- Active ETFs
- Leveraged ETFs
- Synthetic ETFs

## Key Metrics

- NAV (Net Asset Value)
- Tracking Error
- Expense Ratio
- Liquidity

## Risks

- Tracking deviation
- Liquidity mismatch
- Derivative exposure (synthetic ETFs)
