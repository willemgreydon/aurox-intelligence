# Crypto Market Structure

## Key Characteristics

- 24/7 trading
- High volatility
- Fragmented liquidity
- Exchange-dependent pricing

## Market Participants

- Retail traders
- Whales
- Market makers
- DeFi protocols

## Unique Data Sources

- On-chain metrics
  - Active addresses
  - Transaction volume
  - Whale movements
- Exchange flows
- Funding rates (perpetual futures)

## Market Regimes

- Bull (expansion phase)
- Bear (contraction phase)
- Sideways (accumulation)

## Critical Metrics

- Realized volatility
- Implied volatility
- Funding rate
- Open interest
- Net inflow/outflow

## Structural Risks

- Exchange failure
- Smart contract risk
- Liquidity collapse
